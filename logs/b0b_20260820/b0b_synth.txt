DID ANY SHARED PIPELINED LOOP MOVE?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  No.  Every PIPELINED loop present in more than one solution
  has the same achieved II and the same iteration latency in
  all of them, so a latency difference between solutions is
  the loops that EXIST in one and not the other — which is
  what makes the co-simulation difference attributable.

  In particular norm_cols is unchanged, so the shadow's
  per-position comparison did not reschedule the loop it sits
  in, and `shadow - b2ctl` is the added pass rather than the
  pass plus rh times a norm_cols change.

  Non-pipelined CONTAINER loops that changed, as they must:
    tme_top/accum_rows: b2ctl vs b0b
    tme_top/slide_v: b2ctl vs shadow
    tme_top/slide_v: b2ctl vs b0b
  Their iteration latency is the sum of what is inside them,
  so a pass added to or removed from slide_v changes them by
  definition.  That is the change being measured.
