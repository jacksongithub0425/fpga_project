DID ANY SHARED PIPELINED LOOP MOVE?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  YES.  Shared PIPELINED leaf loops that differ:
    tme_top_Pipeline_norm_cols/norm_cols   latency_min        b2ctl=97 shadow=99   [recorded]
    tme_top_Pipeline_norm_cols/norm_cols   latency_max        b2ctl=3361 shadow=3363   [recorded]

  WHAT THAT MEANS FOR THE HALVES.  norm_cols is the loop the
  shadow's per-position comparison lives in, and it runs once
  per OUTPUT ROW.  So `shadow - b2ctl` is the hoisted pass
  PLUS 2*rh, and `b0b - shadow` is the removal MINUS 2*rh.
  Neither half is the thing its name says it is.

  WHAT IT DOES NOT TOUCH.  b2ctl and b0b agree on norm_cols,
  so `b0b - b2ctl` carries none of this.  The frozen net law
  is unaffected; only its split is.

  Module wrappers that differ (the same question, read from the
  submodule reports rather than the top-level loop table):
    tme_top_Pipeline_norm_cols: b2ctl=99~3363  shadow=101~3365
