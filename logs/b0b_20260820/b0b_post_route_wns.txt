The constrained period and the period the board runs at are
different numbers, and the WNS is against the CONSTRAINED one.

THIS IS A CLOCK PROBE, NOT THE SHIPPING IMAGE.  The opened
design is constrained at 8.000 ns, so the PS7 requested
125 MHz and
Vivado constrains the period printed above.  The shipping
image requests 50 MHz, is constrained at 20.000 ns, and runs
on the board at 31.25 MHz / 32.000 ns (contract §8) because
PYNQ applies the PS7's divisor pair to a 1000 MHz PL PLL
rather than the 1600 MHz Vivado assumed.

So this result says the LOGIC closes at the probed period.
It does NOT say the board runs there: reaching it needs the
PS7 FCLK config changed and the achieved rate re-measured
from Clocks.fclk0_mhz on the board.  Until that measurement
exists, quote this as 'closes at <period> in implementation',
never as a board frequency.

with the shipping image's report.  It is NOT the operating
point this build is about.
