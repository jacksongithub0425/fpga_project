--- Controlled comparison and scope ---
The three phase_s vector files remain byte-identical to the Priority 3 and B1
sessions. The B2 runner is intentionally different: it now rejects non-finite
FCLK reads and fail-closes on the expected HWH VLNV. The `hw` suite adds the
approximately T=38 case, the T=52 case, and maximum DMA geometry.

The .bit differs from B1 (2cd4a2b0... -> 1116891c...) and the .hwh differs
(ffa94282... -> 11abec6a...). Exact bit/HWH hashes plus the retained packaged
IP -> Vivado -> bitstream provenance bind the fabric image. The required
`TermCountB2:hls:tme_top:0.2` value in Overlay.ip_dict checks HWH metadata
consistency only; it is not fabric readback.
